## Skills

### Core — a match here matters most
List the things you would be hired for.

### Strong secondary
Real but not headline strengths.

### Working knowledge — do NOT treat as a strength
Be honest. Anything you have touched but could not own in production. A job
whose *core* requirement is one of these is a **gap**, not a match.

### Not present — treat any hard requirement as a gap
The most valuable section. Clouds you do not use, tools you have never run,
languages you do not write, domains you do not work in. Without this the
scoring will happily rate an Ansible-heavy Azure role as a good fit.

## Scoring guidance

**10** — everything aligns: right role, right stack, right seniority, right
location, weak applicant pool.

**8–9** — clear match on domain and stack, seniority in band, one soft gap.

**6–7** — right family of role but a real gap.

**4–5** — adjacent but off: wrong emphasis, or seniority outside the band.

**1–3** — wrong domain, or far outside the seniority band.

**Penalise hard** when the body contradicts the listing — wrong role, wrong
location, wrong stack. Those listings waste the most time, so they must be
visible.
